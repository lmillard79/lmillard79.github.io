## Principal Hydrologist & Chartered Engineer

I am a hydrologist and chartered engineer with over 20 years of experience specialising in **flood risk mitigation** and **water resource management** — protecting communities and enabling infrastructure development across Australia and internationally (UK, UAE, Saudi Arabia).

I deliver flood risk management solutions and surface water impact assessments that enable major infrastructure projects to proceed. I have helped clients navigate complex approval pathways, protect communities from flood risk, and unlock development potential across diverse sectors including renewable energy, transport, dam safety, and municipal water supply.
